Dashboards & Business Communication

Building a real dashboard (kept tool-agnostic so it transfers to Excel, Sheets, or Python), writing an executive summary, and practicing the question every stakeholder actually asks: "so what should we do."

By the end of this module you'll be able to
  • Build a dashboard a non-technical stakeholder can read in under a minute
  • Write an executive summary that leads with the recommendation, not the method
  • Choose the right metrics to surface, and the ones to leave out
  • Handle "so what should we do" in a live Q&A, not just in writing

Projects

Ten scenarios. Pick your capstone, build your portfolio.

Exercises teach one skill at a time. These ten are full, realistic analyses across ten different industries, messy raw data, an ambiguous business question, and a real deliverable. Complete one of the first four as your Module 9 capstone, then build out the rest as a real, varied portfolio: at least three are SQL-heavy, three are Python-heavy, several are dashboard-focused, and several deliberately require more than one tool.

How grading works here: this is a static, client-side course with no backend, it cannot grade your written analysis or dashboard automatically. Each project gives you a real rubric, sanity checks to verify your own numbers against, and a self-assessment checklist, honest self-scoring, not an automated grader.

Desktop Labs

The in-browser Tableau and Power BI libraries are simulations. They teach judgment. They do not run Desktop. Most junior analyst posts still want hours in one real app. Complete one of the two labs below before the capstone.

This page cannot open Tableau or Power BI for you. Download the dataset, build the view on your machine, then paste evidence here. A made-up URL will not pass.

01

The dataset

Use the course ledger, not a sample Superstore file. Every row is an order with region, product line, units, unit price, and revenue.

Download retail_sales_ledger.csv

What to build

A view a merchandising manager can use in under ten seconds: revenue by region and product_line, with a filter on order_date. One chart or matrix is enough. Do not screenshot a blank start screen.

02

Tableau Public

Free. Publish the workbook, then paste the public URL. Host must be public.tableau.com over https.

  1. Install Tableau Public (or Tableau Desktop if you already have it).
  2. Connect to the CSV. Put region and product line on rows or columns, sum of revenue on text or a bar.
  3. Add order_date as a filter.
  4. Save to Tableau Public. Copy the workbook URL.
03

Power BI Desktop

Free on Windows. Publish to the Power BI service, share a OneDrive/SharePoint .pbix, or paste a service link.

  1. Install Power BI Desktop.
  2. Get data from the CSV. Build a clustered bar or matrix: region, product line, sum of revenue.
  3. Add a date slicer on order_date.
  4. Publish or upload the .pbix somewhere you can share a link.

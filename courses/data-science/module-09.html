Module 9 of 9 · Capstone

Capstone Analysis Project

One full end-to-end analysis, chosen from four realistic business scenarios. Messy raw data through to a written recommendation, a dashboard, and a recorded five-minute findings pitch, the closest simulation of a first week on the job this course offers.

By the end of this module you'll be able to
  • Run a real analysis start to finish, on your own, on data nobody cleaned for you first
  • Choose your own approach to an ambiguous business question
  • Build a dashboard and a written recommendation someone can act on
  • Present a finding and a recommendation the way you would in a first week on the job
In production

The capstone build and pitch exercise are being finished

The four scenario options are already specified in full. See Portfolio Projects for "The Leaky Funnel," "The Understaffed Quarter," "The Campaign That Didn't Work," and "The Slow Season," any of which becomes your capstone and your first portfolio piece.

What the capstone requires
  • Pick one of the four scenarios (or complete more than one for a deeper portfolio)
  • Clean the raw, deliberately messy dataset yourself
  • Produce a written recommendation memo and a dashboard
  • Record a five-minute findings pitch, as if presenting in a first week on the job

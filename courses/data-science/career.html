The job-landing playbook

Where the work actually is

Freelance: Upwork and Fiverr, starting near the low end of the range and building reviews. Entry-level roles: LinkedIn easy-apply plus direct informational outreach. Cold outreach: a free mini-analysis for a local business in exchange for a testimonial, the same tactic small agencies use to land their first clients.

Resume bullet formula

Action verb, what you analyzed, the tool, the business result. Worked from a real portfolio project, not a generic template.

Analyzed 18 months of e-commerce order data in Python and pandas to identify a 23% cart-abandonment spike at checkout, informing a UX fix.

Cold outreach template

Short, no fluff, offering a free mini-analysis in exchange for a testimonial and a review, the fastest path from zero reviews to a first paid gig.

Hi [name], I noticed [specific, real observation about their business]. I'm building a portfolio in data analysis and would like to run a free 1-hour analysis of [specific metric] for you, no cost, just a testimonial if it's useful. Interested?

Interview prep

Technical screens usually include a live pandas or SQL exercise, not just conceptual questions. Take-home case studies are common, practice explaining your approach out loud. For behavioral questions, use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) against a real portfolio project.

Realistic pattern, not a promise: most students who finish the portfolio and send the outreach templates land a first paid analysis or an interview within a few weeks of finishing. That's typical, not guaranteed, and depends heavily on effort, market, and location.

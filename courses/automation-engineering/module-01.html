Your first client workflow

A workflow is not a pile of tools. It is a trigger, a change to the data, and a side effect someone can notice. If you cannot draw that, you cannot sell it.

You will be able to
  • Name the trigger, the transform, and the side effect in a real business request
  • Assemble a graph that starts with a trigger and ends with a notify
  • Reject a graph that loops or skips the trigger
1

The only three parts that matter

A dentist says: "When someone books on the website, put them in the CRM and text the front desk." That is three jobs:

Trigger

Something that already happened in the world. Form submitted. Payment captured. Email received. The workflow does not invent this.

Transform

You change the shape of the data so the next system can accept it. Rename fields. Drop junk. Refuse a missing email.

Side effect

Something the outside world can see: a CRM row, an email, a Slack message, a charge. This is the part that can hurt people if it runs twice.

Agency mistake: starting in the tool picker. Start with the sentence the client said. If you cannot underline the trigger, you are not ready to open n8n.

Do it once

Stripe will send the same event twice. Your client will click Submit twice. If your workflow is not idempotent, you will charge twice and then write a very expensive apology.

You will be able to
  • Define idempotent: same key, same result, one side effect
  • Store a result for an event id and return it on replay
  • Refuse a design that keys on "whatever timestamp we feel like"
1

The replay is not a bug in Stripe

At-least-once delivery means you will see duplicates. That is normal. Your job is to treat event.id as a lock: first time, charge and remember; second time, return the remembered result and do not charge.

Bad keys

"now", the email address alone, or "customer + today" — two legitimate charges the same day collide, or two retries miss each other.

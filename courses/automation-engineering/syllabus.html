Practice and projects

80 graded practice scenarios across four libraries (workflow design, APIs, reliability, AI steps). Options are shuffled. 8 portfolio briefs you rebuild in a real tool and score on the Portfolio page. 2 cross-module challenges. 1 final transfer assessment. The Learning Roadmap shows the whole path on one page: what's done, what's locked, and what to do next.

What the labs are. HTTP, webhooks, and AI steps run in a simulated in-browser runtime. Module 3 uses a teaching hash, not Stripe HMAC-SHA256. You still need n8n, Make, or Zapier on the job — that is homework, not a hidden certificate.

Cross-module challenges

Two scenarios that don't belong to any single module. Both require you to combine idempotency, retries, capacity planning, security, API design, and observability, the same way a real production incident does. Pass both to unlock the capstone.

1

Reliable Payment Pipeline

You're designing a payment pipeline for a client. Make the calls a senior engineer would actually have to make.

System under design
Client→API→Authentication→Payment processing→Queue→Database→Notification

Known failure modes you must design around: duplicate requests (client retries on timeout), network timeouts mid-call, retry storms, queue overload under traffic spikes, invalid/forged signatures, and partial failure (payment succeeds but notification fails).

Architecture
A client's request times out after the payment actually succeeded server-side, and the client retries. What's the correct design to prevent a double charge?
Reliability
Under a burst of timeouts, all clients retry with the same fixed 2-second delay. What happens, and what's the fix?
Security
A payment webhook arrives claiming to be from your payment processor, but you haven't verified anything about it yet. What must happen before you trust its contents?
Capacity
Normal traffic is 40 payments/min, each queue worker handles 12/min, and your SLA requires ρ < 0.8. A flash sale is expected to triple traffic. What's the minimum defensible number of workers to provision for the sale?
API design
You need to add a "refund" endpoint. Which contract properties are non-negotiable given everything above?
Observability
Payment succeeds but the notification step silently fails (a partial failure). What must be true of your telemetry for you to even find out this happened?

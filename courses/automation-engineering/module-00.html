What this course actually is

You will learn to put AI inside a business workflow that survives retries, replayed webhooks, and a model that does not do what you asked. This is not an n8n click-along, and it does not promise you an agency.

1

Who this is for

Career switchers, freelancers, and operators who can use email and a spreadsheet, and who want to get paid to connect tools. You do not need to write Python. You do need to read an error message without panicking.

No programming required Comfortable with forms, email, and a CRM idea Willing to debug a failed run
Honest fit: if you want a computer-science course on queueing theory, the old Automation Engineering notes are not this product. If you want a video of someone clicking n8n, YouTube is cheaper. This course grades whether your automation is safe to sell.
2

What you will be able to do

Map a process

Turn "when someone submits the form, we should…" into a trigger, a transform, and a side effect.

Talk to other systems

Read JSON, call an API, verify a webhook, and refuse a request with a bad signature.

Survive failure

Retry a timeout without sending three invoices or charging a card twice.

Bound the AI

Treat the model as a step with a schema and a human gate, not as the owner of the workflow.

3

What this course will not do

It will not get you a job. It will not certify you on n8n, Make, or Zapier. Those UIs change. The failure modes do not. n8n is the example platform in the career pages, not the identity of the course.

It will not teach you to train models. If you want to build agents in code, that is the AI Engineering course.

4

How you are graded

Each module needs a hands-on lab and a checkpoint quiz at 80%+. The capstone unlocks after Module 7 and two cross-module challenges. Practice libraries are extra reps; they are stored in this browser only and do not yet sync across devices.

Progress for modules syncs to your account when you are signed in through Zenith Lab. Practice-task ticks do not.

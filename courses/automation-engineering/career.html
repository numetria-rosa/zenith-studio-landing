Career Path Edition

How people actually get paid for this

This course does not get you a job. It gives you a portfolio of workflows you can demo and a clear split between freelance integration work and W-2 ops/revops roles.

Read this as a map, not a promise. Upwork-style AI integration work is where paid demand grew fastest in 2025–2026. "AI Automation Engineer" is a rarer W-2 title than Data Analyst.
1

What this course covers vs still needed

You practice here

Process mapping, JSON, webhooks, retries, idempotency, bounded AI steps, approval gates, client-shaped briefs.

You still need on the job

A real tool (n8n / Make / Zapier), one CRM you can click, basic DNS/hosting if you self-host, and the ability to write a one-page scope.

2

Three honest paths

Freelance / small agency

Sell a lead pipeline, inbox triage, or booking follow-up. Charge for the system plus a monthly watch. Your portfolio is a running demo and a written failure-mode list.

Ops / RevOps / automation specialist

W-2 work inside a company that already pays for HubSpot, Shopify, or a helpdesk. They want someone who will not double-send. Titles vary.

Not this course

AI Engineer who writes RAG and evals in code — that is the AI Engineering course. SOC analyst — that is not this catalog.

3

Pricing a first project (directional)

Public 2026 operator writeups put simple single-trigger builds in the hundreds of dollars and multi-step AI-in-the-middle builds in the low thousands. Treat those as hearsay until you have your own invoices. Do not underprice a refund-capable agent as if it were a Zapier two-step.

4

Interview / sales questions you should be able to answer

  • What happens if this webhook is delivered twice?
  • What is the AI allowed to do, in verbs?
  • Who gets paged when the CRM returns 401 for an hour?
  • What will I hand you besides the n8n JSON?

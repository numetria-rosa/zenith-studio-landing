AI Code Detective

Plausible AI-generated code. Find the real defects, spare the innocent lines, then prove the fix.

Two phases per case. First the charge sheet: select every real defect and nothing else. Selecting everything fails, because a reviewer who rejects all eleven hunks is as useless as one who approves all eleven. Then the proof: fix the code so hidden tests pass. Spotting a bug and being able to prove you fixed it are different skills, and only the second one ships.
Why the decoys are there
  • Every case includes lines that look suspicious and are fine — a style preference, a naming quibble, a performance worry that does not apply at this scale
  • Charging them costs you the case, because in a real review three genuine findings mixed with four preferences get discounted as a whole
  • Reading the explanation on a decoy you charged is the most useful thing on this page
These cases are one of four capstone practice tracks. You need 3+ passed cases here or you can still clear the bar with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (3+ in at least three of the four tracks), plus both Desktop Labs.

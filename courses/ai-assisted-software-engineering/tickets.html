Why it is a board and not a syllabus

Real work does not arrive as “Chapter 4: Arrays”. It arrives as a person with a problem, usually described badly, often with a deadline attached. Every module here starts from the sentence Priya or Dan actually said, and part of the work is deciding what they meant.

NL-001 through NL-014 each walk the apprenticeship loop on the page: predict, inspect, then prove it with the graded exercise. Simulations are labelled. Cursor does not run in this tab. NL-014 is prompt engineering, inserted after the web modules so you can use it in Cursor, review, and capstone work.

Northline Digital and Northline Clinic are invented. The tickets are not: every one is a shape of request you will get, including the vague one and the one that is already someone else's half-finished AI-written pull request.

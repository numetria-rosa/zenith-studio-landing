What it does

Everything you sell on day one.

One agent that answers, qualifies and hands off, with a simple dashboard for your team.

Core agent

  • Answers FAQs from your knowledge base: packages, prices, dates, hotel distance to the Haram, what’s included, cancellation policy.
  • Replies in the customer’s language: English, Arabic, Turkish, and Urdu.
  • Instant replies 24/7 with a natural, human-sounding tone.
  • Never invents prices or availability. If it’s not in the knowledge base, it hands off.

Lead capture

  • Asks qualifying questions: travel dates, number of travellers, budget, departure city, hotel tier.
  • Saves every lead to a simple dashboard with the full chat history.
  • WhatsApp alert to the agency owner when a hot lead comes in.

Human handoff

  • One-tap takeover from the dashboard or from your own WhatsApp.
  • Auto-handoff on complaints, payment disputes, visa problems, and anything the AI is unsure about.
  • The AI pauses itself as soon as a person replies.

Self-serve setup

  • Sign up and send us your WhatsApp number - we connect it for you.
  • Paste or upload your packages and FAQs.
  • Test it in a built-in chat simulator, then go live.
  • Priced in GBP, paid by card. One monthly plan with a message cap.

The basics

  • Business hours and away-message mode.
  • Tone and language settings.
  • Simple analytics: conversations, leads captured, response time, handoffs.

What it will never do

  • Quote a price, date or seat that isn’t in your knowledge base
  • Handle a complaint, payment dispute or visa problem on its own
  • Keep talking after your team steps in

FAQ

Questions agencies ask.

Will it ever make up a price?

No. It only answers from the packages and FAQs you add. If a price, date or availability isn’t there, it tells the customer a team member will follow up and hands the chat to you.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Arabic, Turkish, and Urdu. It replies in whatever language the customer used for that message.

Can my team take over a conversation?

Yes, in one tap from the dashboard or straight from your own WhatsApp. The AI pauses itself as soon as a person replies.

When does it hand off automatically?

On complaints, payment disputes, visa problems, and anything it isn’t sure about.

How do I connect my WhatsApp number?

You send us your WhatsApp Business number and we connect it on our side, usually within a day - no technical setup on your end. Then add your packages, test in the simulator, and go live.

How do I pay?

One monthly plan priced in GBP, paid by card. Each plan includes a monthly message allowance.

Who pays WhatsApp’s message fees?

Your agency does, directly to Meta, on your own WhatsApp Business account. Add a payment method in Meta Business Suite when we connect your number. From 1 October 2026 Meta charges for replies sent inside the 24-hour window as well as for template messages, while the first 72 hours after someone taps a Click-to-WhatsApp ad stay free. Zenith Studio’s £49 plan covers the software only, so there is no extra Meta markup from us.

FAQ

Questions firms ask.

Does the Billing Clerk bill clients on its own?

No. It reconstructs your time from your calendar, email and documents and drafts entries with real narratives. Your team reviews and approves them before anything goes on an invoice.

Why does the 14 day window matter?

Invoices sent inside 14 days see write-downs around 6%. After that, write-downs run closer to 18%. Getting entries drafted quickly is how the Billing Clerk keeps you inside that window.

How does the Text-Back decide who gets a consult?

It asks the questions from your case criteria, such as practice area and jurisdiction, and only books consults for callers who fit.

Which leads does the Follow-Up Clerk work?

The leads that did not retain on the first call. You already paid to get every one of them, and most firms never touch them again.

How long does setup take?

Setup is free, and we connect the AI team to your tools within 24 hours of you starting your plan. Your dashboard shows the exact time your setup will be ready, so you are never left guessing. Text-backs start as soon as US carriers approve your number, usually within a few business days. We file that registration for you, and every missed call is emailed to you in the meantime.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no contract. Cancel from your dashboard whenever you want, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. This offer and this pricing are only available through Zenith AI.

How does quarterly billing work?

Pay every 3 months and save 25%: $810 a month, billed as $2,430 each quarter. Monthly billing is $1,080 a month, 10% off the original $1,200.

What do you need from us to get started?

Access to the calendar, email and documents the Billing Clerk works from, your case criteria, and the phone line you want covered. We go through all of it on the first call.

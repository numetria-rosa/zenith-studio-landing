FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do my clients need to create an account?

No. Each client gets their own private link. They open it, tap Upload next to each document, and that is it. It works on any phone.

Who can see the documents?

Only you. Files are stored privately and can only be opened from your signed-in dashboard. The client's link lets them upload, not browse what is stored.

Will the emails look like they come from you?

They are sent in your firm's name, and any reply goes straight to your own email address.

Can I stop chasing a client?

Yes. Pause or resume any client in one click, or delete them along with every file they uploaded. Chasing also stops by itself once everything is in.

What if a client sends the wrong thing?

Send it back with a reason, like "wrong year" or "pages are missing". It goes back on their list with that reason, and the next reminder asks for it again.

What does it cost?

$120 a month for your whole client list. Setup is free and you can cancel anytime.

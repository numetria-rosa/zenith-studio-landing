FAQ

Questions people ask.

Does it replace my receptionist?

It answers every call your team can't get to, so nothing gets missed. Most clients use it for after-hours, busy stretches, or as the only line.

Can it actually book appointments?

Yes. It checks your real calendar on Cal.com and confirms on the call itself, not just a message for you to follow up on.

What happens if it can't help someone?

It transfers to the fallback number you set, or takes a detailed message for you to follow up.

How long does setup take?

You're live in 2 to 7 days. We connect your number, your calendar, and tune your hours and FAQs.

What does it cost?

A one-time $360 setup, then $300 a month.

Do I keep my existing phone number?

You get a new dedicated AI Receptionist number. Forward your existing line to it whenever you want calls answered.

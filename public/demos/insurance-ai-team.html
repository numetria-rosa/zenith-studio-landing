FAQ

Questions agencies ask.

Do I have to switch my AMS?

No. Document Audit gives you the named insured, coverage limits, dates and red flags, ready to copy into the AMS you already use.

Which CRMs does it work with?

The CRM & Logging agent connects to the CRM you already use, so there is nothing new for your team to learn. We confirm your setup on the walkthrough call.

What's the difference between "Live today" and "Built and tested"?

The Intake Agent is live today and runs on your existing AI Lead Capture & Follow-Up service. Document Audit, CRM & Logging and Renewal Reminders are built and tested, and are set up for your agency after you sign up.

Will clients know they're texting with an automated reply?

The Intake Agent sends a real, conversational text that answers what the person asked, not a canned autoresponder. It keeps the conversation moving until your team takes over.

How does quarterly billing work?

Pay every 3 months and save 25%: $637.50 a month, billed as $1,912.50 each quarter, instead of $2,550 a quarter on monthly billing.

What do you need from us to get started?

An export of your book of business for Renewal Reminders, and access to your CRM so the AI agents can log leads automatically. We walk through both on the first call.

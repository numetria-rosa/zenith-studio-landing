FAQ

Questions before you pick.

Which service should I start with?

If you run an insurance agency, a law firm or a brokerage, start with the AI team built for it: each one covers the jobs that usually cost you the most. If email is what eats your day, AI Inbox Manager works for any business. Not sure? Use the helper above or book a walkthrough.

Do I have to change my software?

No. The agents connect to the tools you already use, like your inbox, phone line, calendar and CRM. Nothing to install and no new app for your team.

How fast can I be live?

AI teams are connected to your tools within 24 hours, and your dashboard shows setup progress as it happens. AI Inbox Manager is live within 24 hours. Texting starts as soon as US carriers approve your number, usually within a few business days, and we file that registration for you.

Is there a contract?

Every AI team comes with free setup, live within 24 hours, and you can cancel anytime. Each service page also has a live demo and a guided tour of the dashboard.

Can I combine services?

Yes. You can add AI Inbox Manager to any AI team. Each service is priced on its own, and all of them show up in the same client dashboard.

Where are these prices available?

The launch prices and free setup are only available through Zenith AI.

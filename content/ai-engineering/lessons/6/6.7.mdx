Test 3 makes `primary_call` raise a non-retryable error, with `max_retries` set to 5. If your code checks `attempt >= max_retries` without ALSO checking `retryable` first, what happens?

<Answer>

Without checking retryable first, a non-retryable error would keep getting retried until the attempt counter separately reaches max\_retries, 6 total calls to a request that was guaranteed to fail on the very first one. The retryable check needs to short-circuit independently of the attempt count, a non-retryable error should stop the primary loop on attempt 1, regardless of how high max\_retries is set.

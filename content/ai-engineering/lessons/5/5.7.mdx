Test 4 feeds your loop 4 different actions in a row before a final answer on step 5. If your repeat-detection logic doesn't reset the counter when the action CHANGES, what false failure could this test trigger?

<Answer>

Without resetting on change, an off-by-one counter bug could accumulate across DIFFERENT actions and falsely trigger the stuck condition even though nothing actually repeated, incorrectly cutting off a loop that was making real progress. The counter has to be scoped to "how many times in a row has THIS SPECIFIC action just repeated," which means it must reset to 1 (not 0) the moment a different action appears.

## What this module assumes

Module 4 made a single tool call safe to execute. This module is about what happens when a model doesn't stop after one call, it uses a tool's result to decide what to do next, calls another tool, and keeps going until it has an answer, or until something has to stop it.

## What's new

Control flow you don't fully control. The model decides how many steps it takes and what each one is, your code's job is to bound that process so it can't run forever or spin on a failure.

## What's assumed

<Assume>

Modules 1-4 completed. Comfort with loops, arrays, and functions that return different shapes depending on what happened.

</Assume>

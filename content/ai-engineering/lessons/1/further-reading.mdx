<Muted>If you want to go deeper than the browser can take you:</Muted>

- Most major model providers document a native "structured output" or "JSON mode" feature that constrains generation at the token level, worth using when available, it reduces but does not eliminate the need for your own validation.
- JSON Schema (json-schema.org) is the standard vocabulary for describing the shape this module's toy `{required:[...]}` schema is a simplified stand-in for.
- In production, this shows up as the boundary between "the model" and "the rest of your system", the validator is that boundary, and it's the same architectural role a request-validation layer plays for any external, untrusted input.

### Notes on methodology
- Each listing was opened individually on Google Maps and/or Yelp and confirmed to have **no "Website" link** displayed (only phone, address/service area, and hours).
- Rockford towing leads came from Yelp, where the businesses appear as "Unclaimed" (or lightly claimed) profiles with no linked site.
- Junk removal, roofing, tree service, and landscaping searches in all five cities turned up almost entirely businesses that already have websites — these niches are heavily saturated with SEO-savvy operators in these markets. Towing, mobile mechanics, and handyman/home-repair services consistently had the highest share of no-website, phone-only operators, which is why they dominate this list.
- "Likely mobile" / "dispatch line" labels are inferred from business model (solo operators vs. multi-truck towing dispatch), not confirmed by carrier lookup — worth a quick text-vs-call test before a mass campaign.
- 28 businesses total across the 5 cities.
